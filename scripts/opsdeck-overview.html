OpsDeck
Operations platform for
creator-commerce agencies

One system that runs an entire affiliate agency — brands, people, client reporting, pay and performance.

Replaces the usual pile of spreadsheets, chat threads and calendar reminders with a single place where work is assigned, done, reviewed, rated and paid for.

Why it exists

The problem

  • Client reports live in scattered docs with no review trail
  • Nobody can say who is accountable for which brand
  • Bonus calculations are manual, late and disputed
  • Performance reviews rely on memory, not evidence

What OpsDeck does

  • Every brand has one named owner and one coordinator
  • Reports follow a fixed approval chain, end to end
  • Pay is calculated from data the team already enters
  • Scores are built from attendance, output and reviews

How a week runs

1 · Clock inShift starts, daily tasks appear
2 · CheckpointPer-brand numbers prepared
3 · Team meetingPresent, get rated, take actions
4 · Client reportDraft → verified → approved
5 · Month endIncentives verified and paid

Core modules

01Brands & clients

  • Every brand with its owner, coordinator, tier and status
  • Monthly revenue, ROI and sampling goals per brand
  • Each brand reports in its own currency
  • Temporary cover with an expiry date when someone is away

02Client reporting

  • Weekly, bi-weekly and monthly reports
  • Fixed sections: performance, creators, videos, ads, products
  • Draft → submitted → verified → approved, named at every step
  • Star-rated by the reviewer; ratings feed performance
  • Share a read-only link with the client, no login needed
  • Export to Word or PDF

03Attendance & leave

  • Clock in and out, breaks, office or working from home
  • Forgotten clock-outs auto-close and ask for acknowledgement
  • Correction requests routed to the team lead
  • Leave requests with an approval chain and paid/unpaid split
  • Monthly attendance % from real records, holidays excluded

04Performance

  • One score per person per month, from four weighted pillars
  • Review scores, incentive completion, attendance, conduct flags
  • Bands: promotion · good · warning
  • Green and red flags with a written reason and an author
  • Simulator to test weighting changes before applying them

05Incentives & pay

  • A monthly plan per person, built from brand-linked targets
  • Four sources fill themselves in: attendance, team roll-up, ad revenue, commission tier
  • Commission tier pays a % of revenue above a benchmark, converted to local currency at a set rate
  • Verify → pay → freeze: a paid month never recalculates
  • Salary, history and total payout in one view

06Weekly meetings

  • Each team has its own meeting day, time and link
  • A live presentation queue: start, present, rate, finish
  • Per-brand checkpoint decks prepared beforehand
  • Meeting ratings roll up into the monthly score
  • Action items become tracked tasks with owners

Core modules, continued

07Tasks

  • Assigned per person, optionally tied to a brand
  • Daily, weekly and monthly recurring, resetting on their own
  • Priorities, due dates, comments and attachments
  • Work reassigns automatically when someone goes on leave

08Team & access

  • Eight roles, each with its own navigation and permissions
  • Access enforced in the database, not just hidden in the interface
  • Org chart, lead reassignment and brand switching tools
  • Deactivating someone preserves everything they produced

09Knowledge base

  • Standard procedures and how-to articles
  • Proposed, reviewed and approved before publishing
  • Acknowledgement tracking: who has read what
  • Visibility per role or per person

10Notifications

  • In-app and push, on desktop and phone
  • Per-category preferences per person
  • Snooze or mark read straight from the notification
  • Reminders for reports, meetings and clock-ins

11Analytics

  • Per-brand goal tracking, month on month
  • Revenue, ad spend, sampling and creator output in one view
  • Cross-channel lift analysis
  • Client portal for read-only sharing

12Integrations & admin

  • Pulls shop data to fill reports automatically
  • Ad account linking for spend and per-video results
  • An assistant answering questions from the team's own procedures
  • Full data export as a single backup file

Access is real, not cosmetic

Permissions are enforced by the database on every request. An Ads Manager signed in on their own laptop cannot retrieve a brand they were not granted, even outside the app. Hiding a menu item is not the same as denying access — here it is both.

Also worth knowing

  • Runs in any browser; installs as an app on desktop and phone
  • Survives unreliable connections without losing work
  • Live updates — changes appear without refreshing
  • Sensitive changes recorded with who and when
  • Paid and approved records frozen against later edits
  • Light and dark themes, per person
