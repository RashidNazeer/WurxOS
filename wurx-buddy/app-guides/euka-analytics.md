## What this page is for
Live TikTok Shop performance, creator and content numbers pulled straight from Euka, for one Euka account at a time. Open it from the sidebar: **Euka Analytics** (Boss only).

## Who can use it
Only the Boss. Every other role is sent back to the dashboard.

## How to choose what you are looking at
1. Pick the Euka account in the dropdown at the left of the filter bar (the shop icon). Switching it clears the numbers shown for the previous one.
2. Pick a range: **7 days**, **30 days**, **90 days** or **Custom** (then choose start and end dates). The chosen dates show on the right as "start → end".
3. Click **Refresh** (top right) to force fresh numbers from Euka instead of recently cached ones.
4. Choose a section tab: **Overview**, **Creators**, **Products**, **Content & Ads**, **Funnels**, **Campaigns**, **Export**.

## What each section shows
- **Overview:** headline figures (Total Shop GMV, Affiliate GMV, Orders, Ad Spend, Impressions, Creators Reached, Active Creators, Samples Approved, Video Conv. Rate, Avg Views / Video, Revenue / Creator, Post Rate) with change vs the previous period; a daily GMV and orders chart; Sample approval rate, Featured products and Livestream GMV.
- **Creators:** Top creators by GMV, Top videos by revenue, Creator level breakdown, Creator outreach funnel.
- **Products:** the weekly report preview (below) and Top products by GMV.
- **Content & Ads:** Content overview (videos, views, likes, comments) and Ads overview (boosted videos, coverage, boosted GMV, ROAS).
- **Funnels:** Affiliate funnel, Creator outreach funnel, Conversion funnel.
- **Campaigns:** Campaign breakdown (videos, revenue, revenue per creator, reach, response rate).

## How to preview a weekly report from Euka (Products tab)
1. In **Weekly report — Euka preview**, pick a **Week anchor** date. It snaps to the Sunday that starts that week; the week label appears next to it.
2. Click **Generate preview**.
3. The weekly-report layout appears marked "Preview · not saved". Filled in: GMV, Affiliate GMV, Orders, top creators, products by affiliate GMV and units. Left blank: ROI, Shop Score, Samples, Videos Posted, Top videos, GMV Max, Off-site.
Nothing is saved — real reports are still created by the APC in Reporting.

## How to export raw data (Export tab)
Click one of **Creator videos**, **Sample requests**, **Creator level**, **Collab invites**, **Spark codes**. A JSON file for the current store and date range downloads.

## Rules & gotchas
- Euka counts days in US-Pacific time; WurxOS report weeks use Pakistan time, so totals on the edge days can differ by up to a day.
- The page uses the first store of the chosen Euka account; there is no separate store picker.
- If Euka can't be reached you'll see "Couldn't reach Euka: …"; a single block that fails shows "Failed: …". Try **Refresh**.
- Numbers are cached for about 10 minutes unless you click **Refresh**.
