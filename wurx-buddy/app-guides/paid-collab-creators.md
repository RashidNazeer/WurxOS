## What this page is for
A card list of every paid-collab creator deal: who the creator is, which brand and product, the deal, payment and video status, who hired them and when. Open it from **Paid Collab → Creators** in the sidebar.

## Who can use it
Boss, Operation Leads and Paid Collab Team Leads. Everyone sees the same list; the page is read-only for all roles.

## How to move between months
1. The page opens on the **current month** (by hiring date).
2. Use the left and right arrows to go to the previous or next month, or **Today** to jump back to the current month.
3. Click **View All Time** to see every deal; click **Back to month view** to return to monthly view.

## How to filter the list
1. Type in **Search name, brand, product…**.
2. Narrow further with the drop-downs:
   - **All Brands** — one brand.
   - **All Payments** — Paid, Pending or Not yet.
   - **All Videos** — Done, In progress or Not yet.
   - **All Hired By** — the person who hired the creator.
3. The line above the cards shows how many creators and brands match.
4. If nothing matches you see "No creators match" — clear the filters or change the month.

## How to read a creator card
- Name, brand and a TikTok icon (click it to open their TikTok profile; it is greyed out when no handle is recorded).
- **Deal** and **Product**.
- A payment pill and a video pill.
- Footer: who hired them and the hiring date.

## Rules & gotchas
- Nothing can be edited here. Deals come from the paid-collab tracking sheet; update the sheet, then click **Refresh** to reload it (data is otherwise cached for about 10 minutes).
- The month is the creator's hiring month, not when videos were posted or payment was made.
- A creator with several deals appears once per deal.
- A status the sheet leaves blank is shown as "Not yet".
