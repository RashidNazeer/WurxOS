## What this page is for
Configure the weekly agenda meetings: each team's recurring day, time and Google Meet link, which guest teams sit in, and who is on each guest team. Open it from **Agenda Meetings → Settings**. It has three tabs: **General**, **Schedules** and **Guest teams**.

## Who can use it
Boss and Operation Lead only. Other roles don't see the menu item and are sent back to the dashboard if they open the link.

## How to set the fallback Meet link (General)
1. Open the **General** tab.
2. Paste a link into **Permanent Google Meet Link**. It is used only for a team that has no link of its own.
3. Click **Save settings**.

## How to schedule a team's meeting (Schedules)
1. Open the **Schedules** tab. Each Team Lead's team is listed with its APC count.
2. Pick the meeting day (or **Not scheduled**) and the time.
3. Paste the team's own Google Meet link.
4. Under **Guests**, tick the guest teams that should attend this team's meeting. Each shows how many people it reaches.
5. Click **Save schedules**.
6. If this week's meetings were already notified, you're asked **Apply to this week?** — choose **Only upcoming weeks** or **Apply to this week too**. Only the teams you changed are updated.

## How to manage guest-team members (Guest teams)
1. Open the **Guest teams** tab.
2. Click **Add person** on a team and choose someone from the list. They are notified of meetings the team is already invited to.
3. To remove someone, click the cross on their chip and confirm. They stop seeing and being notified about those meetings.

## Rules & gotchas
- Teams left **Not scheduled** are skipped when teams are notified and don't appear on Upcoming Meetings.
- The link, time and guest boxes stay disabled until a day is picked.
- A team is a Team Lead plus the APCs who report to them.
- A person marked **GUEST** with no APCs attends other meetings and has no meeting of their own to schedule.
- Members marked **BY ROLE** join a guest team automatically because of their role; they can't be removed here (change their role instead).
- Being on a guest team grants nothing by itself — the team must also be ticked into a meeting under Schedules.
- A "Guests are double-booked" warning means one guest team is ticked into two meetings at the same day and time. It warns but doesn't block.
- To change guests for one week only, use the pencil on Upcoming Meetings instead.
