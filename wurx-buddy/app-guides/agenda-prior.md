## What this page is for
The history of weekly agenda meetings: completed meetings and "missed" ones (scheduled but never started), with attendance, evaluations and remarks. Open it from **Agenda Meetings → Prior Meetings**.

## Who can use it
Everyone in the Agenda menu. What you see depends on your role:
- **APC:** your own team's meetings, and inside each one only **Your evaluation** (overall summary and task reviews for you).
- **Team Lead:** your team's meetings with **Attendance**, every APC's evaluation and **Team Lead remarks**.
- **Boss and Operation Lead:** every team's meetings, everything above, a **Presentation timeline**, and a **Rate** / **Edit** button per APC to score the week.
- **Guests (e.g. Paid Collab TL, IPC, Ads Manager):** the meetings their guest team attended, as a read-only record including the timeline.

## How to find a meeting
1. Use the month navigator (arrows) at the top right to pick a month; the reset button returns to this month.
2. Choose **Newest first** or **Oldest first** in the sort box.
3. Meetings are grouped by **Week N** (numbered the same as in Upcoming Meetings). Each card shows the team, date and time (PKT), and a **Completed** or **Missed** badge.
4. Click a card (**View record** or **Rate this week**) to open it. Click **Back to Prior Meetings** to return.

## What a meeting record shows
- Date, scheduled time, week number and actual duration.
- **Attendance** — Present, Absent or Not marked per APC (not shown to APCs).
- **APC evaluations** — per APC: Presented (with order) or Did not present, the overall rating and summary, and each task review with rating and notes. Click a name to expand it.
- **Team Lead remarks** — the OL's rating and remark for the TL (not shown to APCs).

## How to rate an APC's week afterwards (OL / Boss)
1. Open the meeting. The header shows how many APCs are rated, e.g. "N/M rated".
2. Click **Rate** (or **Edit** if already scored) next to the APC.
3. If they weren't recorded as presenting, click **Mark as presented** so it's on record.
4. Set the four sliders (Daily task quality, Overall workflow, Response time, Efficiency). They save automatically ("Saved" appears). Click **Done**.

## Rules & gotchas
- Missed meetings appear here so the OL can still score that week for the team.
- **Mark as presented** only fixes the record; it has no score or salary effect on its own.
- If the rating box shows "Trial mode", the score is saved but does not yet affect the official monthly score.
- An APC on a guest team still sees only their own evaluation for their own team's meetings.
- New finished meetings appear automatically, with no refresh needed.
