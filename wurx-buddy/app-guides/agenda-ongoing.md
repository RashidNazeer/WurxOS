## What this page is for
The live room for a weekly agenda meeting: join the Google Meet call, mark attendance, take turns presenting, and (for OLs) review each APC. Open it from **Agenda Meetings → Ongoing Meetings**, or it opens automatically when an OL starts a meeting. Everything updates live for everyone, with no refresh needed.

## Who can use it
- **APC:** lands in their own team's room when it is live. Gets **Start Presenting** / **Stop Presenting** and a list of their agenda tasks.
- **Team Lead:** lands in their own team's room. Marks each APC **Present** (tick) or **Absent** (cross), and can press **Start** on a pending APC's chip to start their presentation for them.
- **Boss and Operation Lead:** pick any live or paused room from **Live & paused rooms**, or start the next team from **Next up this week**. They get **Pause**, **Resume**, **Finish Meeting**, **Mark presented**, **Remarks**, **Reopen** and the evaluation panel.
- **Guests (e.g. Paid Collab TL, IPC, Ads Manager on an invited guest team):** pick from the rooms they were invited to and watch as observers. They can't mark attendance or run the review.

## How to join and present (APC)
1. When your team's meeting is live, click **Join Meeting Now** to open the Google Meet call.
2. When it's your turn, click **Start Presenting**. The banner shows you as **Currently presenting**.
3. When done, click **Stop Presenting**. Your button changes to **Presented**.
4. If the button says **Locked**, another APC is presenting; if it says **Paused**, wait for the OL to resume.

## How to mark attendance (Team Lead)
1. Open the room while your team's meeting is live.
2. In **Attendance**, click the tick (Present) or cross (Absent) next to each APC.
3. If an APC starts talking without clicking, press **Start** on their chip in **Presentation progress**.

## How to run the meeting (OL / Boss)
1. Open a room (or press **Start Meeting** under **Next up this week**).
2. While an APC presents, the left side lists their agenda tasks. Click **Review Task** on each, pick a rating (**Excellent**, **Well Explained**, **Satisfied**, **Needs Improvement**, **Incomplete**), add notes, and click **Save review**.
3. On the right, fill **Team Lead remarks**, the APC's **Overall summary**, and the **Weekly performance rating** (Daily task quality, Overall workflow, Response time, Efficiency). These save automatically.
4. Click **Mark presented** on the banner to end an APC's turn if they forget to stop.
5. To add remarks later for someone who already presented, click **Remarks** on their chip. **Reopen** lets them present again.
6. Click **Finish Meeting** when done. If present APCs never presented, a warning lists them; **Finish meeting anyway** marks them Presented with no review.

## Rules & gotchas
- Only one APC can present at a time.
- A TL or APC only ever sees their own team's room; if it isn't live the page says "You'll see your team's meeting here the moment it starts."
- If there's no **Join Meeting Now** button, no Google Meet link is set for the team — an OL adds it in Settings → Schedules.
- The weekly performance rating may show "Trial mode" — the score is saved but doesn't yet count toward the official monthly score.
- The TL's reporting star is no longer rated here; it's given when a report is approved.
- **Pause** keeps the meeting open for everyone; **Finish Meeting** ends it for everyone.
