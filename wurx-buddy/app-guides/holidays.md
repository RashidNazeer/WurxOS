## What this page is for
This is where the Boss marks company-wide days off, such as Eid. A day marked here counts as present for everyone. Leave that overlaps it is not charged against anyone's quota. Open it from the sidebar with **Holidays**.

## Who can use it
Boss only. Other staff can't open this page, but they see its effects:
- Holiday names appear in the **Range breakdown** when you request leave.
- Holidays are counted in the **My attendance** card on Attendance.
- In the Roster calendar, holidays are shown as already credited.

## How to add a holiday
1. Click **Add holiday**.
2. Enter a **Label**, for example Eid-ul-Fitr.
3. Pick the **Start date** and **End date**. For a single day, use the same date for both. The end date moves forward automatically if it's earlier than the start.
4. Optionally add **Notes**.
5. The box shows how many calendar days the holiday covers, and how many are weekdays (Saturdays and Sundays are skipped).
6. Click **Add holiday**.

## How to edit or delete a holiday
1. Each holiday in the list shows its dates and day count.
2. Click the pencil icon to open **Edit holiday**, change the details, then click **Save changes**.
3. Click the bin icon and confirm to delete it.

## Rules & gotchas
- The label, start date and end date are all required. The end date must be on or after the start date.
- Adding, editing or deleting a holiday changes attendance coverage and leave charging for everyone on those dates. The Attendance and Performance pages pick up the change straight away.
- Holidays count as present for the whole team, including people who didn't clock in.

## Related pages
- Attendance (`/attendance`): holiday days count toward everyone's monthly coverage.
- Leave (`/leave`): holiday days inside a leave range are not charged.
- Performance (`/performance`): the attendance pillar includes holidays.
