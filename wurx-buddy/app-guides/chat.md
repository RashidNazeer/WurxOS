## What this page is for
Internal messaging: one-to-one chats and group chats with anyone in the company. Open it from **Chat** in the sidebar.

## Who can use it
Every role. Everyone can message any active user. You only see conversations you are a member of.

## How to start a one-to-one chat
1. Click the **+** button (New chat) next to **Conversations**.
2. Search in "Search people by name, email, role…".
3. Click the person. The chat opens straight away (if you already have a chat with them, it reopens).

## How to create a group chat
1. Click **+** (New chat).
2. Tick the round checkbox on the right of at least 2 people. The window switches to **New group chat**.
3. Optionally type a **Group name (optional)** (otherwise it is called "Group").
4. Click **Create group (N)**.

## How to send and manage messages
- Type in the box at the bottom and press **Enter** or click **Send**. Use **Shift + Enter** for a new line.
- Hover a message to **Copy** it; on your own messages you can also **Delete** it.
- Unread counts appear next to each conversation; opening a conversation marks it as read.
- Use "Search chats…" to find a conversation by name.
- The list is split into **Direct** and **Groups**.

## How to leave or delete a conversation
Click the three-dot menu (Conversation options) at the top of the chat:
- One-to-one chat: **Delete chat** removes all messages for both of you.
- Group: **Leave group** stops you receiving messages; other members keep the group.
- Group created by you (or any group, for the Boss): **Delete for everyone** permanently removes the group and all its messages.

## Rules & gotchas
- Chats are text only; there are no file attachments.
- Messages cannot be edited — delete and resend instead.
- Only the latest 300 messages of a conversation are shown.
- A chat labelled **Left the chat** means the other person is no longer in it.
