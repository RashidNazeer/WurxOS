## What this page is for
Shows the APCs who report to you and how many brands each one holds, so you can see who is busy and who is free. If the Boss allows it, you can also onboard new APCs here. Open **My team** in the sidebar.

## Who can use it
Team Leads only. The **Add APC** button appears only if the Boss has given you the "Can add APCs" permission.

## How to check your team
1. The subtitle shows "N APCs · X managing brands · Y available".
2. Each card shows the APC's name, email and either a brand count (e.g. "2 brands") or **Available** if they have no brands.
3. Use **Search by name or email…** to find someone.

## How to add an APC (with permission)
1. Click **Add APC** (or **Add your first APC** when the team is empty).
2. Fill in **Full name** and **Work email**. A **Password** is generated; click **Regenerate** for a new one or type your own (at least 8 characters).
3. Optionally add **Responsibilities** tags.
4. Click **Create user**.
5. On the "User created" screen, click **Copy credentials** and share them with the new APC, then click **Done**.
6. The new APC automatically reports to you and appears on your team.

## Rules & gotchas
- Only active APCs whose manager is you are listed.
- Without the permission you see "Add-APC permission not granted"; ask the Boss to add APCs for you or to grant the permission.
- You cannot set permissions or choose a different manager when adding an APC.
- The **Hire date** field in the add form is only saved when the Boss creates the account; ask the Boss to set it afterwards.
- Cards are view-only: you cannot change an APC's brands or manager from this page. Moving an APC to another TL is done by an Operation Lead or the Boss.
