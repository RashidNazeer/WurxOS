## What this page is for
Company documents, SOPs and policies, each stored as a link (Google Docs, Drive, Notion, videos, etc.). Open it from **Knowledge Base** in the sidebar.

Documents are grouped into categories: **Delivery Roadmap**, **BootCamp Curriculum**, **Operational SOPs**, **Training SOPs** and **Policies** (plus **All**).

## Who can use it
Every signed-in role can open it, but there are two views:
- **Boss and Developer** get the management view: add, edit, delete, approve proposals, see who has read each document, reply to comments.
- **Everyone else** gets the reading view: read, mark as read, comment. **TLs and OLs** can also propose new documents.

Which documents you see:
- Boss, OL and Developer see every document.
- Others see approved documents shared with **Everyone**, with their role, or with them personally.
- Documents can be shared by role only with Team Leads, Operation Leads, Paid Collab TLs, APCs and IPCs, so Ads Managers only see documents shared with Everyone or with them by name.

## How to read and acknowledge a document
1. Pick a category tab, or search with "Search documents…". A red dot on a tab means it has unread documents.
2. Click the document title to open the link in a new tab. For video links, click **Play Video** to watch inside the page.
3. Click **Mark as Read** when you have read it; the card turns green and shows **Read**. Click **Undo** to reverse it.
4. For SOP categories, use the version dropdown (**All Versions**, **Latest Only**, or a specific version). The newest version of a document shows a **Latest** badge.

## How to comment
1. Click the speech-bubble button on a document.
2. Type in "Add a comment or suggestion…" and press Enter or the send button.
3. Boss replies appear under your comment as **Boss Reply**.

## How to propose a document (TL, OL)
1. Click **Propose Document**.
2. Fill **Title**, **Link / URL**, an optional **Description**, and pick a **Category**. SOP categories also need an **SOP Version** (e.g. v1.0).
3. Click **Submit for Approval**. It goes to the Boss, who decides who can see it when approving.

## How to manage documents (Boss, Developer)
- **Add Document**: Title, Link / URL, Description, Category, SOP Version (for SOP categories), and **Visible to**: **Everyone**, **By Role** or **Specific Users**. Click **Add Document**.
- **New Ver.** (SOP categories): adds a new version of the same document.
- **Reads**: shows who has read the document (filter all/read/unread).
- Pencil to edit, bin to delete, speech bubble to see comments, **Reply** or delete them.
- **Pending Submissions** lists proposals: **Approve** (then choose visibility and click **Approve & Publish**) or **Reject** (optional reason).
- **Bulk Import**: upload or paste a CSV (columns title, url, description, tab, version, visibilityType, roles, userEmails); **Download template**, **Parse & validate**, then **Import N rows**.
- **Check Duplicates**: scans for documents with the same link or title.

## Rules & gotchas
- A link that is already in the Knowledge Base cannot be added or proposed again.
- SOP documents need a version; BootCamp Curriculum documents do not.
- Proposals are not visible to others until the Boss approves them.
