## What this page is for
A private what-if tool. It shows how your monthly composite score is built and lets you test scenarios, such as "what Performance score would get me to 90?". Nothing you do here changes your real score or salary. It isn't in the sidebar: open **Performance → My Performance** and click **Simulate my performance**.

## Who can use it
OL, TL, PCTL, APC and IPC, each for their own score only. Boss has no personal score, so Boss can't open it. It always uses the current month.

## How to understand your score
The **How your score is built** card explains the four pillars, their weights and how your role's Performance pillar is calculated:
- **TL (when the TL method is live):** 60% your team's average score, plus 40% reporting. Reporting is your OL's weekly star rating plus report accountability.
- **APC (when weekly ratings are live):** 60% weekly checkpoint, plus 40% external report. The external report is your TL's star per report plus report accountability.
- **Everyone else:** the average of five manager ratings from 0 to 100.

## How to try a scenario
1. The controls under **Try a scenario** start from your real numbers.
2. Change the **Performance** controls:
   - sliders for each rating,
   - **Add week** or **Week** to add a what-if week,
   - star sliders,
   - **Reports verified (N)** together with **Deductions (D)** (TL) or **Returns (D)** (APC).
3. **Bonus & Incentives**:
   - Switch **I have an incentive plan this month** on or off.
   - Set **Plan completion**.
   - Switch **OL has verified my incentives** on or off.
4. **Attendance**: set **Monthly coverage**.
5. **Monthly Flags**: set how many **Green flags** and **Red flags**.
6. The **Simulated composite** updates live, next to **Your current** score and the difference between them. **What's driving it** shows each pillar's contribution.
7. Click **Reset to my real numbers** to start over.

## How to reach a goal
In **Reach a goal**, set **I want my composite to be**. The card then shows the Performance pillar score you'd need if everything else stays as you set it. If Performance alone can't get you there, it says so and tells you the maximum you could reach.

## Rules & gotchas
- If you have no incentive plan that month, its weight is shared among the other three pillars.
- An incentive item counts as complete at 90% or more of its target.
- If you have an incentive plan, your real score stays hidden until your OL verifies it.
- Flags start at 80. Each green flag adds 10 and each red flag takes off 20.
- Levels: 90+ Promotion, 70+ Good, 50+ Warning, below 50 Termination.
- The explainer text always says "40%" for Performance and "25%" for incentives. The pillar chips show the actual current weights.
