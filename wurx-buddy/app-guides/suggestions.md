## What this page is for
A shared idea board for improvements and feature requests for WurxOS. Anyone can post an idea and upvote other people's. Open it from **Requests → Suggestions** (developers have **Suggestions** as a top-level menu item).

## Who can use it
- **Everyone** sees all suggestions, can post new ones, upvote, edit their own while still New, and delete their own.
- **Developers and the Boss** also get a **Review** button on every suggestion to set its status and write a response, and can delete any suggestion.

## How to post a suggestion
1. Click **New suggestion**.
2. Pick a **Category**: New Feature (default), Improvement, Bug Fix, UI / UX or Other.
3. Enter a **Title** and a **Description**.
4. Click **Submit**.

## How to upvote
Click the arrow box on the left of a suggestion. Click it again to remove your vote. You can vote once per suggestion. The list is ordered by most votes, then newest.

## How to edit or delete your suggestion
- **Edit** appears on your own suggestion only while its status is **New**. Change the fields and click **Save**.
- The bin icon deletes it (you will be asked to confirm).

## How to review a suggestion (developers and Boss)
1. Click **Review** on the suggestion.
2. Click a status: **New**, **In Review**, **Planned**, **Implemented** or **Rejected**.
3. Write **Developer notes (shown to submitter)** if you want to explain the decision.
4. Click **Save**. If the status changed, the submitter gets a notification.

## Rules & gotchas
- Everyone can read every suggestion, including who posted it — keep them professional.
- The developer's reply shows on the card as **Dev response**.
- Once a suggestion moves past New, the author can no longer edit it (they can still delete it).
- Use **All suggestions** / **My suggestions**, the search box and the status / category filters to narrow the list. The cards at the top count Total, New, Planned, Implemented and **My ideas** for the current view.
- Something broken rather than an idea? Report it under Bug Reports instead.
