## What this page is for
Shows this month's weekly agenda meetings as week cards, one row per team, and is where Operation Leads notify teams and start meetings. Open it from **Agenda Meetings → Upcoming Meetings**. All times are Pakistan time (PKT).

## Who can use it
- **Boss and Operation Lead:** see every scheduled team. On the current week they get the buttons: **Notify all teams**, **Notify this team**, **Re-notify team**, **Start Meeting**, **Resume**, **Reopen**, **Open room**, and a pencil to change a meeting's guests.
- **Team Lead:** sees only their own team's row. No buttons.
- **APC:** sees only their Team Lead's team row. No buttons.
- **Paid Collab TL, IPC, Ads Manager (and anyone on a guest team):** see only the meetings their guest team was invited to. They can press **Open room** on a live meeting they were invited to, but cannot start or notify. If they are not invited anywhere, the card says "Nothing scheduled for you."

## How to read a week card
1. Each card shows "Week N", the Monday–Sunday date span, and a badge: **Current Week**, **Completed**, **Past** or **Upcoming**. Only the current week has buttons.
2. Each row shows the team (by Team Lead name), number of APCs, that team's own meeting day and time, the APC names and any guest teams.
3. The dot on the right is the meeting status: **Not notified**, **Scheduled**, **In progress**, **Paused** or **Completed**.

## How to notify teams (OL / Boss)
1. On the current week, click **Notify all teams** at the top right. A message confirms how many teams were notified.
2. To notify one team only (for example a team added later), click **Notify this team** on its row.
3. To ping a team again before starting, click **Re-notify team** on a row that is **Scheduled**.

## How to start or continue a meeting (OL / Boss)
1. On a **Scheduled** row, click **Start Meeting**. You are taken to Ongoing Meetings in that team's room.
2. The scheduled time is only a guide; you can start any team early. A caption "you can start it now" appears if the slot hasn't arrived.
3. **Resume** continues a paused meeting; **Reopen** (with a confirm) restarts a completed one; **Open room** enters a meeting already in progress.

## How to change guests for one week (OL / Boss)
1. On a current-week row that has been notified, click the pencil next to the guests.
2. Tick or untick guest teams and click **Save guests**. This changes this week only; the recurring schedule is untouched. Newly invited guests are notified.

## Rules & gotchas
- A team only appears once it has a schedule. OLs see a warning "N of M teams have a meeting schedule. Unconfigured teams are skipped" when some are missing; set them in Agenda Meetings → Settings → Schedules.
- The Start button only appears after the team has been notified for the week.
- Your team is decided by who your Team Lead is. If you see "No meeting scheduled for your team", your Team Lead has no schedule yet.
- Only the current month is shown; past months are in Prior Meetings.
