## What this page is for
An AI assistant that answers questions about using WurxOS (leave, attendance, reporting, and more), and where the Boss trains it. Open it from **Assistant** in the sidebar (just above Notifications).

## Who can use it
Only the Boss during the test phase — other roles are sent back to the dashboard and have no menu entry. The Boss sees two tabs at the top right: **Chat** and **Train**.

## How to ask the assistant a question
1. Open the **Chat** tab.
2. Type in "Ask the WurxOS assistant anything…" and press Enter (Shift+Enter for a new line), or click the send button.
3. The reply appears as it is written; a status such as "Thinking…" shows while it works. Links in the answer open the relevant WurxOS page.

## How to manage conversations
- The **Chats** list on the left shows past conversations; click one to reopen it.
- Click **New** to start a fresh conversation.
- Hover a conversation and click the bin to delete it.
- The Chats list is hidden on narrow (phone-sized) screens.

## How to train the assistant (Boss)
1. Open the **Train** tab.
2. Under **Knowledge**, click **Add knowledge**, enter a **Title** and **Content** (the steps or policy in plain words), and click **Save**. The assistant answers from these entries.
3. Click an entry (or the pencil) to edit it, the toggle to switch it on/off (disabled entries show **OFF**), or the bin to delete it.
4. Under **Assistant settings (persona & greeting)**, edit **Persona / instructions**, **Greeting** and **Model**, and tick or untick **Assistant enabled**, **Use Knowledge Base** and **Use TikTok Shop Academy**. Click **Save behaviour**. Model changes apply to new messages after saving.
5. Click **Back to chat** to return.

## How to switch the Slack relay on or off (Boss)
In the Train tab, under **Slack relay**, use the **Answer questions from Slack** switch. When on, questions that tag a watched person in a watched Slack channel are answered by the assistant and posted only into the internal destination channel. The switch is disabled while the routing is incomplete. **Recent** lists the latest relayed questions.

## Rules & gotchas
- With **Use Knowledge Base** on, the assistant also answers from Knowledge Base articles, respecting who is allowed to see each article.
- With **Use TikTok Shop Academy** off, answers come only from WurxOS guides and internal SOPs.
- Where Slack answers are posted is fixed and cannot be changed from this page; only on/off is available.
