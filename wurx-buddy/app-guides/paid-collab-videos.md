## What this page is for
Lets you watch the videos paid-collab creators have delivered and copy their ad codes. Open it from **Paid Collab → Videos** in the sidebar.

## Who can use it
Boss, Operation Leads and Paid Collab Team Leads. The page is read-only and looks the same for all three.

## How to find a creator's videos
1. The page opens on the **current month** (by the creator's hiring date). Use the arrows, **Today**, or **View All Time** / **Back to month view** to change the period.
2. Type in **Search creator or brand…**, or pick from **All Brands** and **All Creators**.
3. Each card shows the creator, brand, a TikTok icon, and either "N videos · Click to view" or "No videos yet".
4. The subtitle shows how many creators and videos are in the current view.

## How to watch videos and copy an ad code
1. Click a creator card that has videos, or its **View All Videos** button.
2. A window opens with the creator's brand, product, video count and payment status.
3. Pick **Video #1**, **Video #2**, … from the list on the right. The list also shows each video's ad code ("no ad code" if none).
4. The selected video plays in the window. Its **Ad code** is shown underneath so you can copy it.
5. If the link cannot be played inside WurxOS you get **Open on TikTok ↗** instead; if no link was recorded it says "No video URL provided."
6. Close the window with the X.

## Rules & gotchas
- Cards without videos cannot be opened.
- Nothing can be edited here. Videos and ad codes come from the paid-collab tracking sheet; update the sheet and click **Refresh** (data is otherwise cached for about 10 minutes).
- The month filter uses the creator's hiring month, so a video posted this month for a creator hired last month shows under last month.
- Only TikTok links of the form ".../video/<number>" can be played inline.
