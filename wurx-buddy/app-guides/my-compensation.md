## What this page is for
A read-only view of your own fixed salary, your hire date and every change ever made to your salary. Open it from the sidebar with **My Compensation**.

## Who can use it
- Every employee can see their own record. Nobody can see anyone else's here.
- The Boss has no payroll record. If a Boss opens this page, it shows "No payroll record" and nothing else, and Boss has no menu entry for it.

## How to check your salary
1. The **Current fixed salary** tile shows your salary in PKR and the date it took effect.
   - It says **Not set** if the Boss hasn't entered your salary yet.
2. The **Hire date** tile shows your start date and how many full years you've completed.
   - It says **Not set** if your hire date is missing. Ask the Boss to add it.

## How to read your change history
The **Change history** list has one row per change, newest first. The columns are:
- **When**: the date the change took effect.
- **Reason**: Initial, Annual increment, Promotion, Adjustment or Correction.
- **Change**: the old amount → the new amount, with the percentage change.
- **By**: who made the change.
- **Notes**: the Boss's note to you, if any.

## Rules & gotchas
- You can't edit anything on this page. Only the Boss sets or changes salaries, on the Salaries page.
- Your hire date sets your years completed and your work-anniversary banner on the Dashboard.
- Amounts are always in PKR.
- This page shows only your fixed salary, not incentives or bonuses. Those are on the Incentives page.
