## What this page is for
A shared library of links used in the weekly agenda meeting — documents, images, videos or any URL — either general or tied to a brand. Open it from **Agenda Meetings → Resources**.

## Who can use it
Everyone in the Agenda menu can view resources and click **Add resource**.
- **Edit / delete** (pencil and bin icons) appear only on resources you created, resources for a brand you are assigned to, or resources for a brand you own as Team Lead.
- **Boss and Operation Lead** can edit and delete any resource.

## How to add a resource
1. Click **Add resource**.
2. Choose a **Type**: **Link**, **File**, **Image** or **Video**.
3. Enter a **Name** (required) and the **URL** (required).
4. Optionally choose a **Brand**, or leave "No brand — general agenda resource".
5. Optionally add a **Description**.
6. Click **Add resource**.

## How to find a resource
1. Type in **Search resources…** — it matches name, description and brand name.
2. Use the **All types** dropdown to show only one type.
3. Click the URL on a card to open it in a new tab.

## How to edit or delete a resource
1. Click the pencil on the card, change the fields and click **Save**.
2. Or click the bin and confirm to delete it.

## Rules & gotchas
- Nothing is uploaded here — every type (including **File**, **Image**, **Video**) is just a link. Upload the file somewhere shareable first and paste its URL.
- The Brand list in the form shows the brands you're allowed to pick for your role.
- The list updates live when someone else adds or changes a resource.
- This is separate from the main Resources page.
