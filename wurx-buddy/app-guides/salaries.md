## What this page is for
This is where the Boss sets and reviews every employee's fixed salary, keeps hire dates up to date and sees the full salary change log. Open it from the sidebar with **Employees → Salaries**.

## Who can use it
Boss only. Employees see their own figures on My Compensation.

## How to set a salary for the first time
1. Open the **Active Salaries** tab. The header shows how many employees have a salary set, and how many are still waiting for their first entry.
2. Find the person. You can search by name or email, or use the role chips (**All roles**, OL, TL, PCTL, APC, IPC, DEVELOPER).
3. Click **Set salary**.
4. Fill in the **Set initial salary** box:
   - **Reason**: leave it as **Initial salary**.
   - **Starting salary (PKR)**
   - **Effective from**
   - **Notes (visible to the employee)**: optional.
5. Click **Set salary**.

## How to give an increment or change a salary
1. Click **Review** on the person's row, or on the **Pending Reviews** tab.
2. Pick a **Reason**: **Annual increment**, **Promotion**, **Adjustment** or **Correction**.
3. Enter an **Increment %** (the company default is 20; use a negative number for a decrease) or type the **New salary (PKR)** directly. Each field updates the other.
4. Set **Effective from** and optionally add notes. The **Recent salary changes** section shows the last five changes.
5. Click **Apply increment**.

## How to set a hire date
Click the pencil next to the person's **Hire date**, choose the date, then click **Save**. The hire date drives the years-completed count, work-anniversary banners and Pending Reviews.

## How to use Pending Reviews and Salary History
- **Pending Reviews** lists employees who have completed at least one year but have fewer annual increments on record than years worked. Each row shows how many reviews are due.
- **Salary History** lists every change for every employee, newest first. You can search by name or note, and filter by reason: **All**, Initial, Annual, Promotion, Adjustment or Correction.

## Rules & gotchas
- Saving the same amount as the current salary is refused with "No change".
- Notes are visible to the employee on their My Compensation page.
- The list shows active employees only.
- The Boss is never on payroll.
- Ads Managers are not listed on this page at all, so they can't be given a salary here.
- The list refreshes by itself when any salary changes. The refresh icon forces a reload.
