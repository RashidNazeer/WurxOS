## What this page is for
This is where the Boss and OLs pick each month's award winners, such as **APC of the Month** and **TL of the Month**. You write the winner a short message, set a cash reward and announce it. The winner gets a full-screen celebration they must acknowledge, and the reward is added to their incentives. Open it from the sidebar with **Achievements**.

## Who can use it
Boss and OL. Both have the same controls. A Boss or OL can't win an award themselves.

## How to award a winner
1. Choose the **Award month** at the top right. The page opens on last month, because you crown each month's winner after it ends. There is one card per award type.
2. Under **Winner**, pick someone from the list. Each person is shown with their composite score, or "not rated".
   - If you haven't picked yet, a button can suggest the **Top scorer this month**. It's only a suggestion.
   - Your choice is saved as soon as you pick.
3. Enter the **Reward (PKR)**. It saves when you click out of the box.
4. Write the **Message to the winner** (one line, 100 characters at most) and click **Save message**. Either the Boss or an OL can write it. The winner sees it without a name attached.
5. Click **Preview what the winner sees** to check the celebration.
6. Click **Announce**. The button stays disabled until there's a winner and a message.

## How to follow up
The status pill on each card shows where the award is:
- **Not started**
- **Draft**
- **Announced**: waiting for the winner to open it.
- **Seen**: the winner has acknowledged it.

While it's Announced, **Announce again** re-sends the notification. The reward is only ever paid once.

## Rules & gotchas
- Once an award is announced, the winner, reward and message are fixed.
- An award that has been Seen can't be announced again.
- The reward is added to the winner's incentives for the month in which you click Announce, not the award month.
- If the winner's incentives for that month are already verified, Announce is refused. Un-verify that month first.
- Only eligible roles appear for each award: APCs for APC of the Month, TLs for TL of the Month.
- After acknowledging, the winner sees a "You were … for …" banner on their Attendance clock until the end of the following month.
