## What this page is for
Propose a change to one of the agency's SOPs (Delivery Roadmap, Policies, Operational SOPs, Training SOPs), get it approved by the Boss, and track its implementation. Open it from **Changes** in the sidebar.

## Who can use it
- **TL, OL, PCTL, APC, IPC** get the submitter view: **My Requests** (what you submitted), **Assigned to Me** (changes where you are the Implementation Owner) and the **Submit Change** button.
- **Developer** gets the same submitter view, but Changes is not in the developer menu (open `/changes` directly), and developers cannot be picked as an owner.
- **Boss** gets the review view instead: **Requests** (every change request) and **Change Log**. The Boss view has no Submit button.
- Ads Managers cannot open this page.

## How to submit a change request
1. Click **Submit Change**.
2. Fill in **Change Title**, pick the **SOP Affected**, and optionally tick the **Affected SOP Documents** (Knowledge Base articles of that SOP type).
3. Describe the **Current Process** and the **Proposed Change**. **Expected Impact** and **Risks** are optional.
4. Choose the **Implementation Owner** (the person who will carry it out) and, optionally, a **Priority** (Low, Medium, High).
5. Click **Submit**. It gets a change code (CHG-…) and status **Pending Review**, and the Boss is notified.

## How to update a change you own
1. Open **Assigned to Me** (or My Requests) and click the change.
2. Once it is Approved, an **Update Status** box shows buttons: **In Progress**, **Paused**, **Delayed**, **Completed**.
3. Click the one that fits. When you click **Completed**, the Boss reviews it and marks it **Implemented**.
4. Use the **Discussion Thread** at the bottom to talk to the requester and the Boss.

## How to review a change (Boss)
1. On **Requests**, click a change. The badge on the tab counts requests waiting for you (Pending Review plus Completed).
2. Under **Boss Decision** set **Status**, and if needed **New SOP Version**, **Implementation Owner**, **Implementation Start / End** and a **Comment**.
3. Choosing **Rejected** requires a **Rejection Reason**.
4. Click **Save**. The requester and the owner are notified.
5. When marking **Implemented**, update the version on each affected SOP article in the Knowledge Base yourself; this page does not do it.

## Rules & gotchas
- Statuses: Pending Review, Approved, Rejected, In Progress, Paused, Delayed, Completed, Implemented.
- The Discussion Thread only appears after the change leaves Pending Review, and never on a rejected change.
- There is no edit or withdraw button after you submit — use the thread, or ask the Boss.
- The owner can only change the status while it is Approved, In Progress, Paused or Delayed.
- The **Change Log** tab (Boss) shows an external change-log sheet with search, column filters and a card / table switch.
